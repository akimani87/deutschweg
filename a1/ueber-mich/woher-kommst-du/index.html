Woher kommst du? — Where Are You From? — German for Beginners

Say where you're from — and ask someone else.

Meeting someone new
Ich heiße Amina.
Ich komme aus Nigeria.

How to say where you're from

After her name, Amina says where she’s from: Ich komme aus Nigeria — "I come from Nigeria." kommen means "to come," and aus means "from" — together, Ich komme aus … always names your country or city of origin.

A few more ways to say it:

Ich komme aus Ghana.I come from Ghana.
Ich komme aus Kenia.I come from Kenya.

Quick check

Quick check

Ich ___ aus Äthiopien. A. komme B. heiße C. bin — Answer: A, komme.

How to ask where someone's from

To ask, use Woher kommst du? — "Where are you from?"

Woher kommst du?Where are you from?
In short

You can say where you're from with Ich komme aus … and ask with Woher kommst du?
