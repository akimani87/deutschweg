Du oder Sie? — Du or Sie? Formal and Informal 'You' in German

Learn when to say du and when to say Sie.

🤝
du vs Sie
(informal / formal "you")

When to use each

German has two words for "you" — du and Sie — and choosing the right one is one of the most important social skills in the language. du is informal and personal. Sie is formal and respectful, and always written with a capital S to tell it apart from sie meaning "she" or "they."

du — informalSie — formal
FriendsYour doctor
FamilyA teacher or professor
ChildrenYour employer or supervisor
ClassmatesCustomer-service situations
People you know wellAdults you don't know yet

In practice

The same question, asked two ways:

Wie heißt du?What is your name? (informal)
Wie heißen Sie?What is your name? (formal)
Common mistake: Switching from Sie to du without being invited is considered rude in German-speaking countries — when in doubt, stay with Sie until the other person offers du.
In short

du = friends, family, children, people you know well. Sie = strangers, professionals, and formal situations — always a capital S. When unsure, start with Sie.
