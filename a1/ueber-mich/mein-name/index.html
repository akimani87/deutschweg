Mein Name — My Name in German

Say your name, two ways — and ask someone else's.

A first conversation
Hallo!
Ich heiße Amina.
Hallo!
Ich bin Daniel.

How to say your name

Amina and Daniel just told each other their name — in two different ways. Ich heiße … and Ich bin … both mean "My name is …" here. heißen literally means "to be called," so Ich heiße Amina is like saying "I am called Amina." Ich bin … is simpler — just "I am …" — used for names as naturally as in English. Both are completely normal, native ways to introduce yourself; neither is more correct than the other.

Try it with different names:

Ich heiße Kwame.My name is Kwame.
Ich bin Fatima.My name is Fatima.

Quick check

Quick check

which line introduces a name? A. "Guten Morgen!" B. "Ich heiße Amina." — Answer: B.

How to ask someone else's name

To ask someone else’s name, use Wie heißt du? — "What is your name?" (informally, to one person).

Wie heißt du?What's your name?
Ich heiße Amina. Und du? Wie heißt du?My name is Amina. And you? What's your name?
In short

You can say your name two ways — Ich heiße … and Ich bin … — and ask someone else's with Wie heißt du?
