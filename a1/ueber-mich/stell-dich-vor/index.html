Stell dich vor — How to Introduce Yourself in German

Put it all together — introduce yourself out loud and in writing.

Putting it together

Now put it all together. You know how to say your name, where you're from, and where you live — time to introduce yourself in one go. The shape is always the same: name, then origin, then residence — just your own details each time.

Ich heiße Amina. Ich komme aus Nigeria. Ich wohne in Berlin.My name is Amina. I'm from Nigeria. I live in Berlin.

Say it out loud first — to yourself, or using Sprechen practice. Hearing yourself say it makes the next step easier.

In short

That's a complete self-introduction — the exact shape you'll use for the independent check next, and at the start of the Goethe A1 Sprechen exam.
