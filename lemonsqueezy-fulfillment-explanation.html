DeutschWeg — Digital Product Fulfillment Explanation

How customers receive access to purchased content, and how fulfillment is delivered end-to-end

1. What DeutschWeg is

DeutschWeg is a web-based German language learning platform (deutschweg.de). Learners create a free account, work through structured lessons and practice exercises, and can optionally purchase additional levels of content and an AI-based writing feedback tool. There is no physical product, no shipping, and no manual order processing at any point — every product is delivered automatically, inside the account the customer already has.

3. Sequence of events, from signup to access

Step 1 — Account creation (before any purchase). The customer signs up for a free DeutschWeg account (email + password, via Supabase Auth) and can immediately start using the free A1 content. No purchase is required to create an account.
Step 2 — Customer chooses a paid product. While signed in, the customer clicks "Unlock" on a specific level or on Exam Whisperer for a level. This takes them to our unlock page (freischalten.html), which shows the exact product, its price, and what it includes before any payment is initiated.
Step 3 — Checkout. Our server generates a Lemon Squeezy hosted checkout URL for that specific product, tagging it with the customer's internal account ID so we know which account to unlock. The customer completes payment entirely on Lemon Squeezy's checkout page — DeutschWeg never handles card details directly.
Step 4 — Automatic fulfillment via webhook. The instant Lemon Squeezy confirms the order, it sends a signed webhook to our server. Our server verifies the signature, reads the account ID and product from the order, and writes a single record granting that account access to that product. This happens within seconds of payment — there is no manual step, no queue, and no human review in the fulfillment path.
Step 5 — Access. The customer is redirected back to DeutschWeg after checkout. Their account now has access to the purchased content immediately — the same lesson pages that were previously gated now render normally, because the app checks for that access record on every page load.

4. Timing

Fulfillment is instant and fully automated. From the moment Lemon Squeezy confirms payment, access is granted in well under a minute (typically a few seconds), governed only by webhook delivery latency. There is no fulfillment backlog, no shipping time, and no dependency on staff availability.

5. Refunds

If an order is refunded, Lemon Squeezy sends a corresponding webhook event, and our server automatically removes that access record from the customer's account — again with no manual step required. Refund handling is symmetric with granting access: the same webhook receiver processes both events.

6. Safeguards against abuse

  • Every webhook is verified against Lemon Squeezy's signing secret before anything is processed; unsigned or invalid requests are rejected outright.
  • Product identifiers and price points are configured only on our server, never exposed to or editable from the browser, so a customer cannot manipulate which product they receive or at what price.
  • Access grants are idempotent — if the same order event is received more than once (e.g., a retried webhook), it does not create duplicate access records or duplicate charges.

7. Customer support

If a customer does not see access appear after payment (for example, due to a delayed webhook), support can verify the order in the Lemon Squeezy dashboard and, if needed, grant the corresponding access record manually as a fallback — this is the only point in the entire flow that could involve a person, and it exists purely as a safety net, not as the normal path.
