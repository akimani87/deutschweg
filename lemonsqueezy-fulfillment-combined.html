DeutschWeg — Fulfillment Explanation

Prepared for Lemon Squeezy merchant review

DeutschWeg (deutschweg.de) is an online course that prepares learners for the Goethe German-language exam, aimed primarily at people preparing to move to Germany for study, work, or family reunification.

This document walks through exactly what a customer experiences from the moment they sign up to the moment a paid tier is unlocked, using real screenshots captured end-to-end with a freshly created test account, in the order a real customer would encounter them. It then covers the technical and anti-fraud safeguards behind that flow.

How fulfillment works, in short:
  1. A visitor creates a free account (no payment required).
  2. The account is instantly provisioned with full access to the A1 course tier — 13 interactive modules, an AI tutor, and progress tracking — at no cost.
  3. From the dashboard, the learner can see additional course tiers (A2, B1, B2), each clearly marked as locked and priced as a one-time purchase.
  4. Tapping a locked tier opens an unlock/checkout summary screen showing exactly what is included, the one-time price, and a "Secure checkout via Lemon Squeezy" notice.
  5. Completing checkout through Lemon Squeezy instantly unlocks that tier's modules on the same account — no separate download, shipping, or manual step is required. Fulfillment is immediate and automatic.

Walkthrough, step by step

Step 1 — Marketing / landing page

A prospective learner arrives at deutschweg.de. The homepage explains the product and offers a free path to start ("Start A1 Free") alongside a "Get Started" signup button in the top navigation.

Step 2 — Account creation form

Clicking "Get Started" opens the signup form. Only a name, email address, and password are required. No payment information is requested at signup — the free tier truly requires no card.

Step 3 — Form completed

A test account is filled in (name, email, password) ready for submission.

Step 4 — Instant confirmation

Immediately after submitting the form, the account is created and the customer is shown a confirmation screen: "Welcome to DeutschWeg! A1 is free forever — all 13 modules are unlocked." This confirms that free-tier fulfillment (account + content access) is instantaneous.

Step 5 — Real course content, unlocked and interactive

After a short onboarding flow, the learner lands inside actual paid-quality course content: Module 1, Lesson 1.1 ("Greetings"), with interactive vocabulary, audio playback, comprehension checks, and an AI tutor ("AI Pal") panel that responds by name. This is delivered entirely in-browser with no additional download.

Step 6 — Dashboard showing free vs. paid tiers

Back on the main dashboard, the learner can see the full course structure: the A1 tier they already have access to, and three additional tiers — A2 (€39), B1 (€44), and B2 (€49) — each shown with a lock icon and its one-time price. Nothing is hidden or ambiguous about what costs money.

Step 7 — Locked-module screen

Tapping the A2 tab (a paid, not-yet-purchased tier) shows a clear "Unlock A2" screen stating the one-time price (€39) and a single "Unlock →" call to action. The learner cannot access A2 lesson content until this purchase is completed.

Step 8 — Purchase summary before checkout

Tapping "Unlock" opens a purchase summary: price (€39, one-time, no subscription), what's included (all 12 A2 modules, AI Tutor + AI Pal, Exam Vault A2 access, Goethe A2 exam prep), and a "Unlock A2 — €39" button that proceeds to checkout.

Step 9 — Lemon Squeezy checkout hand-off

Directly beneath the purchase button, the page states "Secure checkout via Lemon Squeezy · one-time payment." Selecting this button opens Lemon Squeezy's own hosted checkout (shown here in Test Mode) to collect payment. On successful payment, Lemon Squeezy's webhook flips the corresponding tier to "unlocked" on the customer's DeutschWeg account, and the purchased modules become immediately available — the same instant, automatic fulfillment already demonstrated for the free A1 tier in Step 4.

Technical fulfillment mechanics

Behind the screens above, fulfillment works as follows:

Checkout. Our server generates a Lemon Squeezy hosted checkout URL for the specific product being purchased, tagging it with the customer's internal account ID. The customer completes payment entirely on Lemon Squeezy's checkout page — DeutschWeg never handles card details directly.
Automatic fulfillment via webhook. The instant Lemon Squeezy confirms the order, it sends a signed webhook to our server. Our server verifies the signature, reads the account ID and product from the order, and writes a single record granting that account access to that product — within seconds of payment, with no manual step, no queue, and no human review in the fulfillment path.
Refunds. If an order is refunded, Lemon Squeezy sends a corresponding webhook event, and our server automatically removes that access record from the customer's account. Refund handling is symmetric with granting access: the same webhook receiver processes both events.

Safeguards against abuse

  • Every webhook is verified against Lemon Squeezy's signing secret before anything is processed; unsigned or invalid requests are rejected outright.
  • Product identifiers and price points are configured only on our server, never exposed to or editable from the browser, so a customer cannot manipulate which product they receive or at what price.
  • Access grants are idempotent — if the same order event is received more than once (e.g., a retried webhook), it does not create duplicate access records or duplicate charges.

Customer support

If a customer does not see access appear after payment (for example, due to a delayed webhook), support can verify the order in the Lemon Squeezy dashboard and, if needed, grant the corresponding access record manually as a fallback — this is the only point in the entire flow that could involve a person, and it exists purely as a safety net, not as the normal path.

Summary

DeutschWeg delivers a purely digital product: interactive lesson content, AI tutoring, and exam-preparation tools accessed directly in the browser on the customer's own account. There is no physical shipping, no manual provisioning step, and no delay between payment and access — fulfillment for both the free A1 tier and each paid tier (A2, B1, B2, and Exam Whisperer) happens automatically and immediately once the corresponding step (signup, or checkout via Lemon Squeezy) completes.
